Frequently Asked Questions for Data Analysts

What technical skills should a Data Analyst include on an ATS resume?

A strong Data Analyst resume should highlight SQL (queries, joins, indexing), Python/R (pandas, numpy), Data Visualization (Tableau, Power BI), Spreadsheet modeling (Excel formulas, pivot tables), and database management (PostgreSQL, Snowflake, BigQuery).

How do I write metrics-driven bullet points for a Data Analyst role?

Focus on business outcomes rather than just routine tasks. For example: "Engineered automated SQL pipelines and Power BI dashboards, cutting weekly reporting time by 15 hours and improving data accuracy across 4 departments."
